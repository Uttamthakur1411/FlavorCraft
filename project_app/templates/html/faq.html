Frequently Asked Questions

Have questions? We're here to help you navigate FlavorCraft.

What is FlavorCraft?

FlavorCraft is an interactive marketplace platform that connects passionate home cooks and culinary creators directly with customers. You can share recipe videos, sell authentic homemade products like pickles and snacks, and receive direct inquiries.

Was this helpful?
How do I become a creator on FlavorCraft?

Click on "Register" in the top navigation bar, choose Creator Registration, and fill in your details along with a profile picture. Once verified, you can log in and start uploading your recipes and food items immediately.

Was this helpful?
How do I contact a creator or place an order?

Every recipe and product page features a prominent "Chat on WhatsApp" button. Clicking this automatically opens a direct WhatsApp chat with the creator so you can negotiate pricing, ask questions, or place custom orders easily.

Was this helpful?
Is it free to use FlavorCraft?

Yes, absolutely! Browsing recipes, discovering creators, and registering as either a user or creator is 100% free of charge.

Was this helpful?
Can I upload my own recipe video?

Yes! Registered creators can navigate to the "Upload Recipe" dashboard section to share video tutorials, detailed ingredients lists, step-by-step preparation steps, and food images.

Was this helpful?
How can I leave a review or rating?

Once you log in, navigate to the Feedback section on any product or creator page to leave star ratings and community reviews about your experience.
